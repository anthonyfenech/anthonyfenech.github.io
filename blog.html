SUBJECT: Reflections on the 2025 Season

DATE: December 5, 2025

TIME: 9:30 AM

As we wrap up another year of Tigers baseball, I've been thinking about what makes this beat so special. It's not just the games or the stats—it's the relationships built over 15 years in clubhouses, press boxes, and everywhere in between.

The stories that don't make it into the paper are often the ones that stick with you the longest. The quiet conversations before batting practice. The jokes shared during rain delays. The moments of vulnerability when a player trusts you enough to open up.

These are the stories I'm excited to share in OFF-THE-RECORD.

SUBJECT: Behind the Scenes of Beat Reporting

DATE: December 4, 2025

TIME: 2:15 PM

People often ask me what a typical day looks like as a beat writer. The truth is, there's no such thing as typical.

Some days you're at the ballpark for 12 hours straight, covering a doubleheader and filing three stories. Other days you're tracking down sources, chasing leads, or sitting through a two-hour rain delay wondering if you'll make deadline.

But that's what I love about it. Every day is different. Every game tells a new story. And after 15 years, I'm still learning something new about this game and the people who play it.
