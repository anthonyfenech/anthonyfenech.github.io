BIO

Anthony Fenech covered the Detroit Tigers as a beat writer for the Detroit Free Press from 2015 through 2020, five seasons that spanned the team's transition from a contending club into a full rebuild, ending with the pandemic shutdown that closed his beat.

His work has been recognized by the Associated Press Sports Editors, where he was a top-10 finalist in Class A for beat writing in 2017 and for best digital package in 2020.

Fenech began at the Free Press in 2007 and worked internships at the Las Vegas Sun, Pittsburgh Post-Gazette and MLB.com. He is a member of the Baseball Writers' Association of America.

OFF-THE-RECORD is his first book.
