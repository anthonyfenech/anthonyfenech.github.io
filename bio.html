Bio

Fenech has worked as the Free Press' Tigers beat writer since 2015. He placed as one of the top 10 beat writers in the country by the Associated Press Sports Editors in 2017.

Fenech started at the Free Press in 2007 by taking prep football scores and carrying longtime bowling writer Matt Fiorito’s chair.

After internships with the Las Vegas Sun, Pittsburgh Post-Gazette and MLB.com, he returned in an official capacity in 2011, becoming the voice of the sports department on social media.

A graduate of Woodhaven High School, Fenech considers himself the world record-holder for most live blogs in Freep.com history.
