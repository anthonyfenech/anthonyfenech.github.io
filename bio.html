BIO

Anthony Fenech covered the Detroit Tigers as a beat writer for the Detroit Free Press from 2015 through 2020, five seasons that spanned the team's transition from a contending club into a full rebuild, ending with the pandemic shutdown that closed his beat.

In 2017, the Associated Press Sports Editors named him one of the top 10 beat writers in the country.

Fenech began at the Free Press in 2007 and worked internships at the Las Vegas Sun, Pittsburgh Post-Gazette and MLB.com. He is a former member of the Baseball Writers' Association of America.

OFF-THE-RECORD is his first book.
