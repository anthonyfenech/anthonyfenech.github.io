Bio

Anthony Fenech covered the Detroit Tigers for 15 years at the Detroit Free Press, earning multiple awards from the Associated Press Sports Editors and National Sports Media Association.

A member of the Baseball Writers' Association of America (BBWAA), Fenech brings insider knowledge and authentic storytelling to his work.

He is the author of OFF-THE-RECORD, a memoir about life as a baseball beat reporter.

Currently based in Lincoln Park, Michigan.
